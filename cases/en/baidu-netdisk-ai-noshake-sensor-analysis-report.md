# Baidu Netdisk shake ads: AI-reversing claims about No Shake and magnetometers remain unproven

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-02
Stage: Needs verification

At 13:38:44 Beijing time on November 2, 2026, Simbao (@lixinbao_X) recounts a same-day The Paper test: Baidu Netdisk's launch-screen shake ad could still redirect after the app was added to the motion-data restriction list in iOS 27.2. The account says the feature launched October 27 and restricts gyroscope and accelerometer access. This collection did not independently verify the OS version, permission implementation or journalist's procedure. The research question is why ads still react to phone rotation after motion data is restricted.

The same post says a developer used AI reversing to hypothesize a 'No Shake' implementation in the advertising framework that uses another sensor when motion data is unavailable. A 14:11:15 candidate explicitly describes a magnetometer replacing the gyroscope; a third at 14:54:03 calls it a magnetometer within location functionality. Neither supplies code, call chains or permission experiments. Repeated circulation does not establish actual magnetometer reads, nor does the No Shake name identify a demonstrated triggered path. AI model, REA, APK/iOS binary revision, functions and original analysis output are absent.

The first post also retains a conflicting explanation from an informed source: this was not intentional evasion, but an adaptation update still under Apple review and expected soon. The journalist's reported redirect, the developer's reversing inference and that explanation are different evidence levels; none is treated as Baidu officially confirming permission evasion. Customer-service advice to disable shake ads in the app is not path validation either. Original lookup confirms the first author's identity. The Paper's original article, the developer's original analysis, devices and traffic logs were not obtained.
