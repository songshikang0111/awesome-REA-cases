# Fortnite Blueprints to Verse: a secondary report of AI conversion of over 20,000 graphs

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-22
Stage: Needs verification

A Chinese post at 00:32:19 Beijing time on November 22, 2026 says Epic used AI to convert over 20,000 Fortnite Blueprint graphs to a roughly three-million-line Verse prototype. The stated motivation is moving visual game logic into model-readable text. The target is migration of existing game logic, not copying all of Fortnite from rendered footage.

The post further claims UE6 will remove Blueprints because models cannot read binary files. No official announcement, source, compilation record or behavior comparison was obtained, so that roadmap is not presented as established. AI tools, conversion coverage, human changes and prototype playability are unspecified. Three million lines do not prove functional equivalence or engineering completion. Retained only as an unverified secondary report of AI-assisted behavior-preserving migration. REA is unconfirmed; no target was run and no official endorsement inferred.
