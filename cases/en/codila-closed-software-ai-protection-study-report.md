# A closed-software protection study retold: codila's 22-page research, estimated costs and conflicting results

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-10
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:28:05 Beijing time on November 10, 2026, codila (@0xCodila) introduces a purported 22-page study by Anthropic, OpenAI and Google researchers on AI investigation of closed software and defenses. The post claims 2.37 billion tokens and over 5,000 hours, estimating $566,000 at public prices rather than actual lab spending. Its breakdown is GPT 5.6 Cyber at $246,800/773.6M tokens, GPT 5.6 Sol at $161,900/506.9M, GPT 6 Astra at $83,400/215.7M, Claude Opus 5.5 at $52,300/512.2M and Mythos 5.1 at $22,000/362.9M. All model names, usage and amounts are relayed claims; model registration and prices were not checked, and the breakdown is not a calculation performed here.

The post says encrypted code and hidden keys reduce Claude from 5.33 to 0.32 out of six; the strongest model without safeguards solves 99% within four attempts; stripping symbols costs weaker models 50–71%; and increased effort moves one model from 5.8% to 55.8%. No paper title, task samples, revisions, budgets, score definition or complete tables were obtained. Scores, success rates and percentage changes are not interchangeable metrics, and arbitrary software is not established as crackable in four tries. A supplementary 02:35:21 reply instead says page 20, figure 5 puts six of eight models at 0.33/6 or below while GPT-6-Astra and GPT 5.6 Sol hold up. This conflicts with the main post's one-model wording; it remains unresolved.

The thread only supplies a shortened paper link; the original PDF and institutional attribution were not verified. Recommendations to scan keys, store them server-side, harden code and repeat review per release name TruffleHog, Infisical, a Cloudflare skill, BLA and O-MVLL. These are suggested tools, not established experimental subjects or proof that every leaked key can be found. This retains a concrete evaluation account and its evidence gaps. REA use is uncertain. No tool was installed, target audited, model run or protection verified here.

Sealed snapshot: 52 likes, 2,117 views, 5 reposts. Over 5,000 hours and $566,000 were not independently checked; the latter is a public-price estimate, not paid cost.
