# RhinoAI: a report of Claude/Codex inspecting geometry and editing Grasshopper definitions

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-22
Stage: Related project

At 02:15 Beijing time on November 22, 2026, Marko (@archi_marko) says McNeel shipped open-source RhinoAI with Rhino 9 and made it available for Rhino 8. In the account, Claude, Codex and other MCP clients control geometry, Grasshopper definitions and plugins. Documented demonstrations reportedly include sketch-to-parametric modeling and reverse engineering existing geometry. The concrete value is recovering editable parametric structure from finished geometry, not established recovery of original design history.

The author highlights agents building and editing Grasshopper definitions and advises inspecting graphs, sliders and logic rather than only the viewport result. This is described as something the author would test, not a completed test. Retrieval confirms attribution, but official releases, source, demos and tolerance measurements were not reviewed. Rhino 8/9 support and open-source availability remain secondary claims. Neither Rhino nor MCP was run and REA involvement is unconfirmed. Included as a related project, not certification of geometric equivalence or production suitability.
