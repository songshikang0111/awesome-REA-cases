A housing-survey photo of a tiny room above a stable on Ritterstraße, captioned with measurements, inspired the team to stand inside it and rebuild the surrounding city from old records. The author describes using a 1910 map for building footprints, 1887 heights for buildings, photo-caption dimensions for rooms, Claude reading the 1917 address book to populate owners, shops and tenants, and an 1884 sewer atlas traced into a subterranean network.
