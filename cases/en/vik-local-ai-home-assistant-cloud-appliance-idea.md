# A local-AI home idea: Vik plans to investigate cloud-dependent appliances for Home Assistant

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-10
Stage: In progress

At 15:58:28 Beijing time on November 10, 2026, Vik | Practical AI & Tech (@uctive) says the next stage's hardware is ready and, when time permits, they will connect local AI, Home Assistant and household appliances, including an air purifier. Cheap smart appliances often depend on manufacturers' clouds, so some will need partial reversing. This records a concrete local-control and interoperability plan, not a claim that AI has already investigated or recovered device protocols.

The author says one ventilation fan is already integrated with Home Assistant over Wi-Fi and door sensors are installed. The old extractor hood over the gas stove has no smart controls; future integration might detect cooking and start it automatically. A scenario based on a recent air-pollution incident would alert occupants to ventilate, switch off heat-pump heating by zone when doors open, prompt them to close doors once air improves, then boost heating before returning to normal. Local voice control, independence from external clouds and hood automation are future directions, not an already deployed complete scenario.
