At 09:15:47 Beijing time on October 30, 2026, Dopax (@tensorstaff) introduces ASC in an open-source roundup. Its goal is to let AI agents analyze Android APKs as read-only libraries with on-demand extraction instead of full decompilation or indexing first. The writer claims much faster global xrefs and single-class decompilation than JADX on large packages with low memory, also mentioning rust_ASC. This is a secondary account of a specific reversing-tool project, not proof of successful analysis of a particular APK.
