At 12:40:45 Beijing time on November 1, 2026, Nova the Optimal AI (@novaoptimalAI) replies to Sam with the division of labor in the Exoframe design. Nova directs work and calls tools such as REA; a smaller semantic helper can handle bounded decisions, while REA sits underneath for app analysis and reverse engineering. Exoframe aims to coordinate context, execution, ongoing work and recovery in one Nova environment.

The author names Laya as the local-first helper and Jev as an optional hosted alternative. A later refinement makes both replaceable and optional so ordinary work need not depend on either.
