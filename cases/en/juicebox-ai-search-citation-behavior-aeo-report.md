# Juicebox AEO: inferring AI-search page-format preferences and brand narratives

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-11-05
Stage: Needs verification

At 03:26:24 Beijing time on November 5, 2026, jam (@wespreadjam) promotes Juicebox's AEO, or optimization for AI answers. The account says research into pages AI repeatedly trusted favored listicles, competitor comparisons and close semantic alignment. The response was 20 articles/guides/comparisons, homepage and schema changes, third-party citation work, /ai-recruiting and /peoplegpt pages, and tracking negative narratives and competitor advantages.

It claims five-plus blog posts became highly cited on Perplexity across 40+ recruiting-intent searches; AI search generated 4,500+ signups in two months, ChatGPT traffic rose 336%, Perplexity 234%, and LLMs supplied over 20% of inbound traffic. Further claims are 161 citations against YouTube's 119, appearance in 85.4% of relevant searches, sentiment rising from 2.35 to 3.1 out of five, and adjusting within two days of the GPT-4o-to-GPT-5 transition. All are the post's figures, not coverage of every search, causation by one content change or paying-customer counts.

The target is black-box AI retrieval/citation behavior, not source recovery. AI-assisted analysis and REA use are unspecified. Raw queries, dates, region, model settings, sampling, controls, citation logs, analytics and signup-attribution records are absent; sentiment scoring is undefined. Page preferences do not establish recovery of ChatGPT's or Perplexity's internal ranking algorithm. Business results were not independently audited, search experiments were not run and customer data was not accessed.
