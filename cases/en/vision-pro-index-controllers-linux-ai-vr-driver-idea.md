# Vision Pro plus Index controllers on Linux: No-self's AI-aided plan and driver-reversing idea

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-10
Stage: In progress

At 22:20:53 Beijing time on November 10, 2026, No-self (@tudou_v) says they want to avoid switching between Windows and Linux, reuse a VR PC for LLMs, images and video, and reverse-engineer and port drivers to Linux if needed. The specified PC has a 7800X3D, RTX 3090 with 24 GB and 32 GB DDR5; peripherals are Vision Pro and Valve Index controllers. Playing Half-Life: Alyx is also a goal.

The post includes an AI planning response referring to shared memory. It identifies existing SteamVR, ALVR and space-calibration routes on Linux, corrects an earlier assumption that Windows should be retained, but explicitly says the complete setup has not been tested on hardware and Linux calibration support is insufficiently tested. It proposes checking headset visuals/audio, both hands, grabbing, loading, turning and repeatability after reboot before removing Windows. llama.cpp CUDA and ComfyUI are plans for sharing the PC with AI workloads, not driver-port results.

The model, driver to reverse, protocol and code are unspecified; no completed port is claimed. Having 24 GB of VRAM does not guarantee arbitrary video-model resolution or speed. This collection did not check current linked-tool compatibility tables, operate hardware or change an OS. It is a concrete hardware idea in progress with proposed acceptance checks, not a ready-to-follow compatibility promise. REA is unmentioned.
