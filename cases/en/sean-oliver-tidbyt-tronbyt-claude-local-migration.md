# Tidbyt off the vendor cloud: Sean Oliver uses Claude for a Tronbyt migration and firmware backup

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-05
Stage: In progress

At 05:15:03 Beijing time on November 5, 2026, Sean Oliver (@SeanOliver) says Claude helped move his Tidbyt display from Tidbyt's servers to his own, running the community firmware Tronbyt from a Home Assistant box. Claude also built its first personal app: a countdown to the next calendar event and the top Things task. This is device migration and interoperability using existing community firmware, not a claim that Claude reverse-engineered or created Tronbyt from scratch.

A candidate reply at 05:15:13 says stock-firmware backups failed at every baud rate until Claude read in 256 KB chunks. The author attributes dropped bytes on long reads to the Mac driver for the display's USB serial chip, without a chip model, driver revision, backup hash or restore test. A supplementary post at 05:15:10 says Claude first rendered three animated layouts in Tidbyt's own Pixlet renderer. Sean selected a large countdown that turns amber at 15 minutes and red at five. That post is supplementary context, not another candidate.

Device generation, configuration, app source, firmware image and migration logs were not obtained. Photos and video were not examined and no device was connected. Leaving Tidbyt's servers does not establish that calendar, Things and model services are fully offline or that stock firmware can be restored losslessly. Migration, display and backup success remain author reports. Kept in progress; REA is not mentioned.

Sealed candidates: main post, 0 likes/43 views/0 reposts; backup reply, 0 likes/9 views/0 reposts. The 256 KB, 15-minute and five-minute figures are the author's.
