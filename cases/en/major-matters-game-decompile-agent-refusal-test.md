Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work

Stage: Analysis test

At 01:38:36 Beijing time on November 4, 2026, Major Matters (@Major_Matters) reports responses to requests such as decompiling an owned cartridge or making a personal Mario 64 PC port from an open decompilation. Claude Code, Codex and bare gpt-5.4 each reportedly accepted 8/8, bare Gemini 3.1 Pro accepted 10/10, and Gemini CLI accepted 14/20 with six refusals.
