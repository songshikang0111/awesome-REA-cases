At 02:07:50 Beijing time on November 25, 2026, Mike Takahashi (@TakSec) describes the tools he gives agents for iOS-app testing. Xcode plus devicectl/CoreDevice connects physical iPhones over USB, installs/launches apps and captures screenshots. Appium XCUITest plus WebDriverAgent inspects UI, taps, types, navigates and repeats tests across phones. Frida and related tools hook running apps, trace methods, inspect data and extract decrypted binaries on a jailbroken research phone. The goals are device/build comparisons, runtime research and identifying attack surface specific to the iOS client.
