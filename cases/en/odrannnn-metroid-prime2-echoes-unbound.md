# Echoes Unbound: AI-assisted expansion of PrimeDecomp into a native Metroid Prime 2 port

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-29
Stage: In progress

At 22:19:29 Beijing time on October 29, 2026, the candidate introduces Odrannnn's Echoes Unbound, bringing the 2004 GameCube title Metroid Prime 2: Echoes to modern platforms on the PrimeDecomp decompilation. Supplementary reading obtained the Odrannnn/MetroidPrime2Port README at commit 4e07d849b43e6ea84fa837f71cc7540b9576d7f8, timestamped 15:33:28Z that day: after the candidate but within the window, not presented as the version already available when posted. The developer says parts of the port were written with AI coding assistants under his oversight, without specifying a model or REA.

The README lists native Aurora rendering via SDL3/WebGPU, MusyX audio, widescreen, frame interpolation, mouse/twin-stick/gyro controls, cutscene and light/dark portal-transition skips, save imports and photo mode. This is neither a GameCube emulator nor static recompilation. Upstream decompilation and runtime contributions are retained, not attributed to AI recovering the entire source from a ROM alone.

Documentation lists Linux, Windows, macOS, Android and experimental iOS, labels the project 0.6.2 and pre-release, and supports specified USA/PAL GameCube and Wii Trilogy images. Users must provide games they have rights to use; no assets are included. The project's MIT terms cover its own platform work, not Nintendo game code; upstream and third-party notices remain separate. A public feature list does not establish playthroughs or compatibility on every platform. This collection did not obtain images, build, execute or recheck tests. Recorded as an in-progress preservation project.

Candidate snapshot: 72 likes, 1,256 views, 10 reposts. Supplementary documentation postdates the candidate but is within the window.
Pre-release. AI assisted parts of the port, not necessarily upstream decompilation. Platforms and features were not independently run.
