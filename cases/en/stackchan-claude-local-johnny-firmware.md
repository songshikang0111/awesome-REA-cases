# Stackchan: Claude-assisted cloud-firmware replacement for local JOHNNY on DGX Spark

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-04
Stage: In progress

At 08:20:06 Beijing time on November 4, 2026, J. Austin McCubbin (@austinmccubbin) says he removed all Stackchan desktop-robot factory firmware to avoid its app sending conversations to the cloud, and had Claude help rebuild it from scratch for his local AI environment. He uses NVIDIA DGX Spark and a Python virtual environment for JOHNNY as a local-agent voice interface.

He reports adding facial recognition, moving face tracking, eyeball expressions and voice-session on/off controls in 30 minutes, with a robot under $200 and an approximately $10,000 AI box. These are author claims, without firmware source, device revision, protocol analysis, accuracy, latency or packet captures. Original retrieval confirmed identity; the photo was not inspected. Always listening without uploading is a privacy claim, not a network audit or proof of fully offline dependencies or complete factory-firmware equivalence. REA is not mentioned. No firmware was downloaded or flashed.
