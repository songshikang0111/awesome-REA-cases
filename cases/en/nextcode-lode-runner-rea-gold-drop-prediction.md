# Eleventh-generation Lode Runner AI: a close REA gold-drop prediction, wrong points and a lower average

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-11-05
Stage: Analysis test

At 17:18:25 Beijing time on November 3, 2026, AI-driven development NextCode (@nangoku100katen) reports an unattended eighth-generation Lode Runner AI using REA to read ROM counters and predict where and when enemies drop gold. One example predicts a drop 198 frames later at F24241, with F24243 observed, described as two frames off. Movement remains like generation seven and level seven still times out.

A new post at 03:07:03 on November 5 describes generation eleven questioning a guidebook technique, checking it with REA and attempting it in live play. The author reports the gold-spitting technique working on level three: F12350 predicted versus F12340 observed, ten frames off. However, the expected +200 points are actually +500, all three level-five attempts fail to trigger it, and the average falls below generation ten. Unattended operation, unofficial status and synthetic voice are author labels. A close time prediction is not a correct score prediction or strategy.

This is hidden-rule research supporting automated play, not game reconstruction. Negative outcomes show that rule clues do not establish better routing, collection or completion. The older author/video attribution was confirmed and the new text gives figures, but ROM revision, code locations, counter derivation, agent source, per-game logs, actual averages and checkable repeated trials are absent. Frames were not independently recounted; neither two nor ten frames is a stable error bound. No video was watched, game run, ROM downloaded or test reproduced.
