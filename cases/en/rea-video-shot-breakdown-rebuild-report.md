The documented metric is shot-duration-weighted production-origin classification: 98.3% tools-only on 15 tuning videos; 16 held-out clips split into nine/seven at 99.6%/98.9% tools-only and 99.6%/99.8% with Gemini; ten WeChat-reencoded clips at 88.6% tools-only, 99.6% vision and 100% agent-reviewed. Transcoding erases noise; a 72-second, 29-shot unboxing was misclassified 35% AI by tools then revised to 97% filmed. These are project tests, not universal detection guarantees; full per-sample evaluation and reruns were not performed.
