# RDNA shader research idea: Sebastian Aaltonen suggests SPIR-V conversion followed by AI variable naming

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-08
Stage: In progress

At 22:06:45 Beijing time on November 8, 2026, Sebastian Aaltonen (@SebAaltonen) discusses GPU-shader reverse engineering. He says recent AI-written PS5 emulators translate AMD RDNA instructions into SPIR-V, and suggests analysts reuse the open tools, translate further into HLSL, GLSL or Metal, then ask AI to name variables. The purpose is turning lower-level GPU instructions into more readable shader expressions to understand existing rendering logic.

This is a research idea with a concrete conversion chain, not a report of completed recovery of a particular game or shader. The post names no emulator repository, revision, decompiler, shader sample, output, naming evaluation or semantic-equivalence test. AI authorship of the emulators also remains attributed to the poster. Translation and readable naming do not recover original source, original variable names or an entire graphics pipeline.

Retrieval on November 9 confirms attribution and wording. Retained as an early idea; the in-progress label does not imply an existing implementation. No tool was downloaded or run here; REA is unmentioned.
