# Stutters synchronized with RGB: a secondary account of 51972's AI-assisted Gigabyte GCC research

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-27
Stage: Needs verification

At 01:32:13 Beijing time on November 26, 2026, UNIKO's Hardware (@unikoshardware) relays a month-long investigation by bilibili's 51972 team into alleged amdip stutter. The account blames a combination of a new Nvidia driver, Gigabyte GCC and a Gigabyte GPU with RGB and a screen, rather than the 9800X3D. GCC means Gigabyte's control software here, not the GNU compiler. Driver 595.71 reportedly changes rendering order to fix intermittent black bars in The Ascent, amplifying time spent by GCC communicating with the GPU microcontroller. The team reportedly matches stutter cadence to lighting effects and uses AI tools to reverse GCC, changing RGB-control contention so commands pass faster.

The source also claims a new GCC release fixes the issue, a replacement tool is planned for the next generation and a Chinese representative discussed staffing of the former GCC team. No first-party version notes or statements establish the fix chronology, personnel claims or all-GPU impact.
