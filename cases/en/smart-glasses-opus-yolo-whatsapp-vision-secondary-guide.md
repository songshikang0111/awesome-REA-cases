# Smart glasses to YOLO: separate the Opus reversing claim from the WhatsApp/OBS video-feed plan

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-14
Stage: Needs verification

At 18:22:39 Beijing time on November 14, 2026, an English secondary post says someone used Opus 5.5 to reverse-engineer smart glasses into a live object-recognition device. The concrete use is sending the wearer's view into a local detector and speaking categories such as vehicles, people and dogs back into the ear. No original author or glasses model is named. This is not forcibly merged with the existing Ray-Ban chess, mahjong-assistant or Android replacement-client projects.

The proposed steps do not demonstrate a private glasses protocol: a WhatsApp video call sends the view to the owner's Mac, OBS captures the call window as a virtual webcam, YOLO detects and tracks frames, and a Python script invokes macOS speech and returns audio through a virtual microphone such as BlackHole. Category/confidence filtering, training custom objects and exporting CoreML/TFLite for phone use are further suggestions. These steps and extensions do not establish an implemented phone version or compatibility with every camera-equipped pair of glasses.

The post claims 80 out-of-box classes, default ByteTrack tracking and operation on an ordinary Mac. Exact model configuration, latency, occlusion tracking, spoken left/right positioning and accuracy are unchecked. Detecting the person class is not identifying every individual, and a dog's assigned cross-frame ID is not proof of reliable re-identification. Open-source/free availability and Opus reversing attribution remain secondary claims without a primary repository or AI process records. An existing video-call route may be integration rather than evidence of breaking glasses communications.

Included as a specific AI-assisted device-modification plan, separating attribution from implementation evidence rather than filling gaps with success claims. This collection installed no YOLO, initiated no calls, collected no faces and executed no code. REA is unmentioned; status remains unverified.
