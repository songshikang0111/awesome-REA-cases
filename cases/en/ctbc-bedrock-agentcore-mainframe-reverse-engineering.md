# CTBC COBOL mainframe reversing: multi-agent gates and a reported PoC from about eight person-days to two

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-15
Stage: Needs verification

An earlier source at 07:14:13 Beijing time on November 11, 2026 headlines CTBC Bank modernizing mainframe reverse engineering through a multi-agent pipeline on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore. Mainframe means enterprise computing, not a PC motherboard; the target is understanding existing bank software.

At 00:13:54 on November 13, Michael shares. (@mikogen) relays the same project in an AI operating-model roundup, citing what he describes as AWS coverage dated November 10: COBOL research with one supervisor agent, seven sub-agents, a quality gate at every handoff and a full audit trail. He reports a same-input proof of concept reducing work per artifact from about eight person-days to about two. Artifact type, sample size and model are unspecified. He attributes much of the gain to decomposition and gates, but explicitly says the PoC did not isolate these factors; this establishes neither causation nor production-wide efficiency. Earlier supplementary retrieval confirmed Michael's attribution and wording.

A new secondary account at 13:56:32 on November 15 calls the artifact a report and supplies an intermediate comparison: about eight person-days, about six with a single AI agent, then about two with seven focused agents and quality gates checked into code. It argues that structure beat a bigger prompt, but gives no report definition, sample, same-input evidence or measurement logs. Its seven agents and the earlier supervisor-plus-seven description cannot be silently reconciled into a single counting convention. The new six-person-day figure is not a controlled causal experiment.
