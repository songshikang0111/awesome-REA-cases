At 08:26:22 Beijing time on November 14, 2026, Chenfu (@chenfu336) relays developer Steve Troughton-Smith recently booting iOS 4 build 8A218, extracted from an iPhone 4 engineering unit, in QEMU. QEMU is a system emulator. The target is supplying the runtime conditions for an old system and exposing an unpublished prototype, not AI writing iOS from scratch. The post describes double-pressing Home to show an Exposé-like grid of background-app cards that can be clicked to switch apps, unlike the later horizontally swiped interface.

According to this account, driver and hardware-support gaps had blocked the engineering firmware, and ChatGPT-assisted emulator writing and debugging made it run.
