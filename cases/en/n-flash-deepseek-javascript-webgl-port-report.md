# Flash game N: andai reports decompiling it and paying $2 for DeepSeek to restore playable frame rates

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-24
Stage: Needs verification

At 08:47:00 Beijing time on November 24, 2026, @tvytlx recounts a web port of the Flash ninja platformer N. Supplementary reading covers linked Hacker News item 50620027, posted by andai at 06:01:09 that day, before this window's start. It is first-party provenance, not a new candidate. The author's 2016 laptop struggled with the 2004 game even in official Flash Player. Reasoning that ActionScript and JavaScript are close and a WebGL shim could replace Flash's drawing API, the author decompiled it and asked DeepSeek to try.

Andai reports playable normal frame rates after $2, plus controller support that is not yet configurable, and links a web page. A later edit notes the official online version uses Ruffle and is still unplayably slow on that device, but has better graphics; the port's ninja looks anemic. The $2 is self-reported model spending, not total labor cost, a universal porting price or work duration. No specific fps, browser, laptop configuration, model version, test suite or full source comparison is supplied. Behavioral equivalence and general AI superiority over emulation are not established.

The author acknowledges possible infringement and calls the old game abandonware. Age, free publicity and that label do not grant copyright permission. This collection provides reports and links only, not the game, decompiled source or assets. HN text was read; the port was not opened or executed, no target downloaded and controller, graphics and performance were not checked. REA is unmentioned; status is unverified.
