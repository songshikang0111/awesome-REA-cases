# Wang's three content skills: analyze reference graphics, articles and videos, then adapt original work

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-22
Stage: In progress

At 19:00:00 Beijing time on November 22, 2026, Wang (@awangev8) describes turning his articles into animated videos, graphic posts and X threads for multiple platforms. The AI reconstruction target is reference works' design and production rules, not platform APIs. He feeds reference graphics, articles or videos to WorkBuddy, also naming Codex, Claude Code and Grok Bot as alternatives, analyzes them image by image, paragraph by paragraph or second by second, and packages reusable skills.

The long post provides three prompts with different uses: extract covers, pagination, information density, typography, colors and spacing from graphics to illustrate articles; infer narrative and language rules from articles to turn videos into articles; extract shots, animation, transitions, subtitles, audiovisual synchronization and technical parameters from video to turn articles into videos. These are components of one author's content-adaptation workflow, not three independently accepted software projects. Prompts demand actual SKILL.md files, executable workflows, self-checks and corrections, while avoiding copied protected assets and invented facts. These are requirements issued to an agent, not evidence every item was tested and completed.

The author describes writing one article daily, distributing AI adaptations eight times plus a human narration for nine feedback points, but also says the example narration has not been edited. Reported readership for a Grok practical topic is 800 for the article, 10,000 for animation and 200 for graphics. These are self-reported cross-platform snapshots, not a controlled trial establishing animation is always better or all nine data points are always available. Retrieval confirms attribution; skill files, outputs, platform analytics and permission records were not obtained. No reference works were downloaded, agents run or content published here. REA is unmentioned. Included as an in-progress broader style/method-reconstruction practice.
