# Instagram Reels: sujal reports Claude-assisted APK research and a Java bounce back to the feed

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-01
Stage: Needs verification

At 21:45:02 Beijing time on November 1, 2026, sujal (@sujshr) says he was spending all day on Instagram Reels and reverse-engineered Instagram with Claude. He connected his phone through USB debugging, extracted the APK and reportedly used about 30 lines of Java to bounce a Reels tap straight back to the feed. He claims no root and no modified app, linking a video and describing the work as open source. This is a personal intervention in an existing app's behavior, not reconstruction of Instagram or its server-side recommendation algorithm.
