# CODEGATE 2026 autonomous agent: KAIST abstract reports 22 of 26 challenges across six categories

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-08
Stage: Related project

At 15:18:59 Beijing time on November 8, 2026, VXCON (@vxresearch) previews KAIST Hacking Lab's Donghyeon Kim and Professor Insu Yun presenting an autonomous agent for CODEGATE 2026 challenges. The goal combines environment preparation, competition management and solving without human intervention. It is an AI-assisted security-analysis project, not complete decompilation of a named commercial program.

Supplementary reading on November 9 finds the official DonghyeonKim abstract, Full-Automatic CTF Agent: Lessons from CODEGATE 2026. It says the system was deployed at the finals, using frontier models including Codex and Claude to solve 22 of 26 unique challenges across six categories, with First Bloods in the General and Junior divisions. Planned discussion includes cross-challenge dependencies, dynamic environments, unintended solutions and automation difficulties, emphasizing experienced researchers' roles in narrowing search, recognizing dead ends and changing direction. These numbers are team claims in a talk abstract, not scoreboard verification by this collection.

Challenge-category lists, reversing/pwn breakdowns, agent traces, repository, model revisions, call costs and official scoreboards were not obtained. Thus 22/26 is not a reversing-task success rate or proof of no intervention throughout the competition. The November 28–29 conference has not yet occurred and its agenda is tentative. The abstract's original date is unstated; it is post-window supplementary material, not a new candidate post. REA is unmentioned. Neither agent nor challenge environment was run here.
