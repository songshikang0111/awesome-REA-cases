# A 1.5 GB PS4 game: ItsYaboii reports hundreds of model-hours without completing one arena

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-08
Stage: In progress

At 12:50:46 Beijing time on November 8, 2026, ItsYaboii (@YaboiBMH) replies to bosh's decompilation account, doubting a twelve-hour result for a large game and describing a different personal project. Codex, other models and many subagents have reportedly put hundreds of hours into an approximately 1.5 GB PS4 game without rebuilding even one of its twelve arenas. This records stalled reconstruction rather than a successful port.

Retrieval confirms attribution and the full wording. Supplementary reading of bosh's older post in the reply chain identifies the disputed report as PC Resident Evil 4, not the title of ItsYaboii's PS4 target. The game name, model revisions, destination platform, division between asset and code work, repository and runnable scenes are undisclosed. Hundreds of hours describes the author's model/subagent effort, not auditable human labor, elapsed time or expenditure. One-twelfth is not a whole-game code-completion metric.

Retained as an in-progress negative progress report. It neither establishes that all models cannot reverse PS4 games nor invalidates the other project's specific results merely through skepticism. REA is unmentioned. No game was downloaded, decompiled or run here.
