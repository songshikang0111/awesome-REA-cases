# Karpathy's HN-curated video site: a Claude-assisted viewing alternative, not recovery of YouTube's algorithm

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-02
Stage: Related project

At 03:47:20 Beijing time on November 2, 2026, a poster characterizes Andrej Karpathy's project as 'reverse engineering YouTube' without source. The described work actually uses Claude to extract videos from public Hacker News discussion and build a personal curated site without watch-time optimization, not analyze YouTube binaries, private interfaces or its recommender implementation. The post claims roughly 43 million comments, over 100,000 discovered videos, about 7,000 surviving an initial filter, smaller Claude agents reading 39,834 comments, and a live site with 6,454 videos across 150 topics in about two hours. Karpathy reportedly supplied a Google key, server and domain manually.

The poster says they gave Claude the blog and About page and derived rules: at least three people recommending a video in three distinct months; exclude jokes, complaints and source-only links; rank recurring recommendation months; use middle-frame thumbnails; keep For You state in the browser without an account. This is a secondhand report of public-information curation and a functional alternative, not replication of YouTube recommendations or a no-tracking audit.

The original blog, About page, source and deployed site were not read here. Counts, two-hour duration, classification accuracy and privacy implementation are unverified. A third party's Claude interpretation is not Karpathy's confirmation or an independent test. REA is not mentioned. This differs from another author's existing REA personal YouTube dashboard. No keys, scraping execution or service login were used.

Candidate snapshot: 13 likes, 301 views, 0 reposts. Forty-three million and 39,834 describe different stages; 6,454 videos/150 topics are unverified here.
