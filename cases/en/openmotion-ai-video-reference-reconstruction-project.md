# OpenMotion: turn reference promos into MOTION.md for agent-led reconstruction and continued editing

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-17
Stage: Needs verification

At 18:11:45 Beijing time on November 17, 2026, Tom Huang (@tuturetom) announces OpenMotion as an open-source alternative to Claude Motion and Jianying. The target is understanding the motion language of established product promos and design templates so Codex, Claude Code, DeepSeek Harness and other agents can create videos from references. This does not establish decompilation of Jianying or acquisition of its original source.

Supplementary same-author thread posts from 18:11:46 to 18:11:50 explain that shot order, text timing, transitions and sound design become agent-readable MOTION.md specifications. Brand assets and editable projects let users substitute their product, copy and footage while retaining a style. The author says agents can plan, generate assets and arrange motion; humans can change timing and rhythm in the same project before export, with Skills, MCP and plugin access. A candidate reply lists MP4, MOV (ProRes), WebM and GIF export plus Apache 2.0. These written claims do not verify formats, license files or compatibility with every agent.

The main post claims 14 days, 470,000 lines of code, promos from over 100 companies, over 300 motion-oriented DESIGN.md files, over 152 design systems, over 20 agents and over 95% fidelity. The supplementary thread describes those resources as over 300 editable templates and over 152 Brandkits, also claiming 74 providers and multimodal bring-your-own-key support. These are retained with their distinct wording, not audited inventories, measured human labor, a promise of free models or defined similarity tests. The 95% figure has no measurement protocol, example set or independent scoring.

The candidate acknowledgments explicitly credit HyperFrames, Hypit, rea-video, GSAP, FFmpeg, OpenChatCut and community methods. This is upstream attribution, not endorsement of every claim by those authors or proof of morluto/rea use. Supplementary thread posts explain the project on the same date and are not extra candidates. Attribution and thread text were checked, but repository code, license text, template assets, demo videos and rendered outputs were not reviewed, and the project was not installed or run. Claims of being first, the best alternative or delivering top results with any agent are not adopted as this collection's conclusions. Status remains unverified.

Main sealed candidate: 265 likes, 19,118 views, 53 reposts, 42 replies. Format reply: 8 likes/1,668 views; acknowledgments: 4 likes/728 views. Engagement is separate from the 95% fidelity claim and is not combined into a project total.
