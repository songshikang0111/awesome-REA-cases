# ONT health checks: neohob reports REA recovery of an ISP-account API for automatic rebooting

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-31
Stage: Needs verification

At 13:07:57 Beijing time on October 31, 2026, Neo@Matrix (@neohob) reports that their home optical network terminal slows after prolonged uptime, while the ISP app offers only manual rebooting, prompting REA research. The author explicitly narrows the result: it is not ONT administrator access, but interfaces dispatched through the ISP center and usable only for their current user. This is not device root, a universal administrator or control of arbitrary accounts.

They say a router-based ONT health check asks AI whether the issue is network-related, then calls the interface to reboot the ONT, without building a separate management console. This is a concrete home-network recovery integration, not an independent finding that rebooting fixes every slowdown. A lookup confirmed attribution and an image attachment, not its contents. No ISP, ONT model, firmware, thresholds, AI model, protocol trace, code or stability tests are supplied. Retained as an unverified author report, not generalized to other accounts or devices. No device access, API call or check execution occurred here.
