# PSP Dock: David reuses FUSA for storage/controller switching and PPSSPP save syncing

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-11-04
Stage: Related project

At 07:08:48 Beijing time on November 4, 2026, David (@bitcoinpendium) says he wanted to contribute to the AI-reversing wave by giving an old PSP a Switch-like dock experience without repeated cable swaps. Supplementary author replies at 07:08:49–07:08:56 explain USB game/save synchronization, turning the PSP into a controller for PPSSPP on a PC, then returning to storage mode to copy saves back. The challenge is switching USB storage and HID without unplugging. It reuses FUSA and other projects, with a Next.js XMB-style front end above PPSSPP, not upscaling the PSP's native video output.

The author calls it PSP Dock, says Windows is done, and plans Linux, Android TV and other emulators. They describe source availability without commercial rights. Sync reliability, conflict recovery, performance and device compatibility are untested here. No handheld was connected or game run; legally obtained games are required.
