# SF2X Online: ESPARTACO discloses AI-assisted decompilation for a Windows fighting-game beta

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-27
Stage: In progress

At 07:19:47 Beijing time on November 27, 2026, Gisleno ESPARTACO (@ESPARTACOleague) says SF2X Online is based on his own decompilation work with AI assistance, but that no AI was used for artwork or music. Supplementary author posts at 07:19:45–07:19:46 identify the target as the 1994 SF2X/Super Turbo arcade fighting game and describe a free Windows release currently in beta and seeking testers, unaffiliated with Capcom. These contextual thread posts are not counted as additional candidates.

The author advertises rollback networking, direct and relay connections, TURBO 3 speed and a modern training mode, explicitly listing Casual, Ranked, Rooms and live spectating as planned. Another post presents frame meters, hitboxes, dummy recording, save states and replay takeover as goals; that wording does not certify delivery of every training feature. The purpose is modern practice and online play for fighting-game players, not AI-generated character assets.

Attribution, full wording and a launch-post video attachment were confirmed. The video was not watched, an implementation repository was not examined, and no installer was downloaded or run. Target ROM revision, byte-matching reports, AI model/sessions, latency tests and feature acceptance are absent. Calling it his own decompilation does not establish independent recovery of all upstream code. Free availability is the author's promise, not permission to redistribute original assets. No game or target execution was obtained; REA is unmentioned. Status remains in-progress, separate from other authors' Super Turbo decompilation or AI card-team Street Fighter projects.
