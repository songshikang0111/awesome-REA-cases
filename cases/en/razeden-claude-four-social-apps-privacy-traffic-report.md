# Four social apps compared: RazeDen reports Claude analysis of seven-day privacy records and traffic, without proving recommendation causality

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-03
Stage: Needs verification

At 19:10:38 Beijing time on November 3, 2026, RazeDen (@razeden0) says Spain-related recommendations followed a spoken discussion about moving there. He reports exporting seven days of iPhone App Privacy Report and routing phone traffic through a MacBook for Claude Code analysis of TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat. This is one comparative audit with a shared device and hypothesis, not four independent rebuilds, and is distinct from kairos's earlier backup/microphone-activation account.

The author reports zero background microphone accesses for all four apps, with microphone use only while recording a story. He also claims 143, 96, 88 and 51 tracking domains respectively over seven days, and decoded location, home IP, other devices on the network, contacts and per-video dwell time. No export, request samples, domain list, TLS-handling method, app revisions or classification criteria are supplied. Contacted domains are not automatically trackers, and privacy-report metadata does not establish every request body. Decoding all requests is the author's claim, not confirmed here.

He attributes the recommendations to a brother visiting on the same Wi-Fi and searching for Valencia, matched through IP, location and contacts. That is a post-hoc association, not platform attribution logs or a controlled experiment. His citation of Northeastern research on over 17,000 apps cannot validate this four-app test; that paper was not obtained in this review. He further says Claude changed tracking, precise-location, contacts, local-network and private-Wi-Fi-address settings, after which the feed felt less informed within two days. There is no device-action log, and multiple settings changed together. Neither AI completion of every setting per app nor causality is established.

Original lookup and full-text reading find only a promise to pin the report-reading prompt later, not an inspectable prompt here. Retained as an unverified privacy-research account, not proof that apps universally avoid covert recording or actually used these data to make these recommendations. REA is unspecified. No phone, private report or proxy traffic was accessed.
