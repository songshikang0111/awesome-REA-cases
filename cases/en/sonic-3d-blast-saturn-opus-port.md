# Saturn Sonic 3D Blast: byte-matching decompilation and a native PC port

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-20
Stage: In progress

On October 16, @TheOneMrMatt confirmed Opus accelerated byte-matching decompilation and said an AI disclosure had been removed because of anti-AI reactions. Historical updates identify Saturn Sonic 3D Blast: July research used SH2 code and frame-by-frame state from a custom emulator; on August 3 the author reported recreating a Hitachi compiler environment with Claude Code, recovering the SSSS.BIN Special Stage engine as C and handwritten assembly with byte matching, while three other executables were blocked by a particular GCC environment. On September 26 they reported 100% decompilation, with regional versions, documentation and porting still required. The October 16, 06:04 update describes a native C/SDL PC port with software rendering and OpenGL presentation, widescreen and Special Stage interpolation, but edge garbage and polygon seams remained. The first release was then planned for November 20 with user-supplied US/EU/JP disc files. At 12:26 the author clarified this was not static recompilation but a native port from claimed fully byte-exact, documented decompiled code. Months versus hypothetical manual years is retrospective estimation, not controlled labor measurement. October 20 evidence about four executables and Fable/Opus remains in metadata.

At 08:12:33 Beijing time on November 20, 2026, the same author, Mr. Matt (@TheOneMrMatt), says he has over twenty years of coding, engine and manual reversing experience and used agentic AI to decompile Saturn binaries from the beginning, aiming for months rather than years. He calls the port essentially a 1:1 translation with enhancements, all game logic native without emulation, running well even on Intel Atom netbooks from 2008, and extensively human-reviewed and tested. This adds author-reported process and named older hardware, not collection-confirmed 1:1 functionality, frame rates, byte matching or complete test coverage.

The new reply does not name the game. It is merged under the existing ID based on the author and known Saturn project context rather than creating an unknown Saturn title. Retrieval of the new post succeeded, but its parent and full thread were unavailable. No new release package, source-matching output, hardware configuration, benchmark or test matrix was reviewed, and no game was watched or run here. Resolution of old visual defects and an actual November 20 release remain unconfirmed. Status stays in-progress; a planned date and author tests do not establish release or independent reproduction.
