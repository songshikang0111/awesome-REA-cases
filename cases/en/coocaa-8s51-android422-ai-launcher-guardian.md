# An Android 4.2.2 Coocaa TV: a reported 12KB AI-written guardian returns Home to Aerial Lite in about 0.6 seconds

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-18
Stage: Needs verification

At 23:13:57 Beijing time on November 18, 2026, @AsyncXBit documents modifying a household Coocaa TV from the 8S51 generation running Android 4.2.2, seeking an alternative to an ad-heavy, awkward launcher. An AI agent helps inspect the model, ports and settings over ADB. The author says CleanYourTV's launcher requires Android 8.0 and would not install, leading to Aerial Lite for Android 4.0–4.4.

The launcher opens, but Home returns directly to a Coocaa component rather than Android's standard launcher picker; full Aerial's guardian needs Android 5.0. After a temporary window-observation loop, the author asks AI for a 12KB accessibility guardian that detects the vendor launcher, waits about one-third of a second and brings Aerial Lite forward. Repeated Home tests reportedly take about 0.6 seconds with a brief flash; it also starts after reboot without a resident computer or ADB connection. Claims of no networking, system-app changes, flashing or root have no code/traffic audit here.

The author retains the on-screen safe-mode label, saying this generation's maintenance mode needs a boot console and uncertain panel parameters make disabling it risky; vendor documents were not checked here. Logs reportedly attribute a TVBox crash to an Android 5 API call rather than the guardian. With a compatible 影视仓 V3 3.0.36 package, only home, settings and QR-push screens are reported working. Content interfaces still need configuration; lawful media access or playback is not established.

The sealed post was read in full and retrieval confirms attribution. Guardian source, device logs and vendor documents were not reviewed, nor results reproduced. This is a separate old-TV behavior adaptation, not generic CleanYourTV firmware rewriting or support for every Coocaa model. REA is unmentioned; no TV was connected or target executed.
