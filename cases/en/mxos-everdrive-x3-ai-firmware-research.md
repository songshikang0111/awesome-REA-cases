# Connecting MXOS to EverDrive X3: Mauro Xavier credits AI with closed-firmware research

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-23
Stage: In progress

At 20:53:31 Beijing time on November 23, 2026, Mauro Xavier (@MXRetroDev) identifies AI's role in MXOS development: Mega EverDrive X3 firmware is not open, so he relied on AI for reverse engineering he says he could not perform alone. Otherwise he estimates years of work and possibly needing the openly documented Mega EverDrive Pro instead. Years is a counterfactual estimate without measured comparison, not evidence that AI compressed years into a particular duration.

Supplementary retrieval of the same thread's 09:05:29 introduction identifies the target and predates this candidate window. MXOS aims to turn a Sega Mega Drive/Genesis into a home computer. Xavier reports operation on original 7.6 MHz 68000 hardware with an EverDrive X3, a command line, windowed desktop, multitasking, keyboard/mouse via BlueRetro, SD long filenames, and environments including MSX-BASIC-compatible BASIC, GW-BASIC-style PC-BASIC, MicroPython and 68000 assembly. He also lists a ten-to-fifty-fold speedup from compiling BASIC to native code, without benchmarks. At 19:05:06, another pre-window reply acknowledges AI use while stressing his own MSX, Amiga and embedded-system experience; he doubts leaving everything to AI would work well.

These are the author's project claims and firmware-research attribution, not proof that every OS feature came from reversal. Access for Patreon supporters is planned once fully ready. Public code, a firmware protocol, differential records, hardware tests and a release package were not obtained. Images were not viewed, paid material not accessed and nothing downloaded or run. Neither model nor REA is specified. Hardware operation and performance remain author reports; status is in-progress.

Sealed candidate: 1 like, 16 views, 0 reposts, 0 replies. Years of estimated work, ten-to-fifty-fold acceleration and hardware capabilities are author reports.
