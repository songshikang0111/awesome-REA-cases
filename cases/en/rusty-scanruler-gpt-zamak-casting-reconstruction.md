# A broken ZAMAK casting: Rusty reports GPT rebuilding a printable copy with ScanRuler

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-06
Stage: In progress

At 12:28:45 Beijing time on November 6, 2026, Rusty (@LieutenantRusty) replies to a 3D-scanning discussion, reporting that GPT used ScanRuler's toolset to rebuild a print-ready copy from a scan of a broken ZAMAK casting. ZAMAK is a family of zinc-based casting alloys. The reverse-engineering target is physical geometry, not software code: deriving a replacement-part model from the visible shape of an existing broken part. This differs in author and target from CNC Kitchen's collected GoPro-clamp demonstration.

That supports an author report of initial geometry reconstruction, not a printed part, installation in the original device or compliance with original tolerances.
