# Swipt's launch-ad Swiplet: an account of Opus research into iOS apps and advertising SDKs

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work

Stage: Needs verification

At 21:48:28 Beijing time on November 6, 2026, 0xjackkk introduces Swipt's first Swiplet, intended to skip launch advertisements in Chinese iOS apps. The post says its developer used ipatool, unfairplay and Claude Opus 5.5 to download and reverse-engineer many popular Chinese-region apps and pre-adapt common advertising SDKs, rather than simply tap fixed coordinates.

The described method identifies ads and skip buttons through the iOS accessibility tree, reacts to events and learns recognition results for faster subsequent handling. Bilibili is an example where closing allegedly still requires nearly half a second after button recognition.
