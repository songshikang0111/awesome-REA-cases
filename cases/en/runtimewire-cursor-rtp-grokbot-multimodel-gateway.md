# A multi-model Grok Bot gateway: RuntimeWire reports reversing Cursor's RTP

Category: Applications and clients · REA use unconfirmed
Record date: 2026-11-02
Stage: Needs verification

At 16:40:01 Beijing time on November 2, 2026, RuntimeWire (@runtimewire) reminds readers that its team previously reverse-engineered Cursor's 'RTP' and made a gateway claimed to support any AI behind Grok Bot, rather than only Grok 4.7. The goal is replacing the Bot's model backend. AI is the integration target; the post does not say AI assisted the protocol research, so rea remains unknown.

RTP is not expanded in the source and is not assumed to mean a standard networking protocol. No endpoints, source, gateway implementation, authentication boundaries, model list or success/failure tests were obtained. Original lookup confirms attribution. 'Any AI' remains the author's compatibility claim, not evidence that every model was tested or that platform permissions and terms allow the integration. No Bot account was accessed, interface called or gateway executed.
