# Let the fridge just cool: a reader proposes stripping unnecessary smart behavior with REA

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-11-14
Stage: Needs verification

At 00:19:15 Beijing time on November 14, 2026, a reader replying to @felipefialho_ argues that some devices need not be smart: added complexity once increased failure opportunities and now increases attack surface. They support using REA to make overly smart hardware simple, illustrating it with a refrigerator that just cools without bothering its owner. This preserves a concrete wish for control over a fridge's basic function versus unnecessary smart behavior, not an established attack on a device.

There is no vendor, model, network feature, ad, telemetry, firmware, protocol or modification result. It cannot be expanded into removal of a particular vendor's cloud lock or RFID filter, or successful offline operation, and is distinct from the existing GE filter idea. No model or actual REA run is specified, and effects on temperature control, safety interlocks or warranty are untested. This collection connected no device and ran no tools; status is an unverified idea.
