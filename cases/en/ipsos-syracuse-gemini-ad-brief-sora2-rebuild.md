# Thirty-second ads from ten brands: reported Gemini brief inference and Sora 2 reconstruction

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-04
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:04:04 Beijing time on November 4, 2026, Pete Sena (@petesena) describes an Ipsos/Syracuse University Newhouse School test in a reply: real 30-second ads from ten brands, including Cheerios, Fiat, H&M and Visa, all made before 2021, were given to Gemini to infer briefs and shot lists, then to Sora 2 to make AI versions. The poster says no human creative touched the process. The reversed target is an ad's creative structure, not an advertising-platform protocol.

Lookup confirms the author, but the original study, ten full input/output pairs, model revisions, prompts, audience measurements and evaluation method were not obtained. The scope of 'no human', output quality, effectiveness differences and copyright permissions are unverified. This does not show AI ads outperform originals or that the entire production process involved no people. Only the secondary report's method and sample scope are retained, not promoted to independent reproduction. REA is not mentioned.
