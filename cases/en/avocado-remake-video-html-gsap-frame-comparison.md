At 19:23:46 Beijing time on November 19, 2026, Wanderson Jackson (@jackson99ai) announces Avocado Remake. It aims to reconstruct reference videos as editable scene-by-scene code, compare frames with the original, then substitute branded products, voiceovers and UGC assets. It does not recover the original editing project.

The author calls reconstruction free and MIT open source, while brand assets consume Avocado credits; the entire workflow is not simply free. These posts supply context, not extra candidates.
