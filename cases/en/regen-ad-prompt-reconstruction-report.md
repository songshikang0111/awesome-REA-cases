At 00:46:21 Beijing time on October 28, 2026, an earlier candidate introduces ReGen as obtaining an ad prompt to remake it for another product. Earlier supplementary retrieval of Amy Hua (@wenyanhh) at 00:39:54 that day confirms a Chrome-extension pitch: infer prompts from visible ads on Instagram, Pinterest, X and other websites, then remake them. This is not reversing those platforms' clients or APIs, and 'extract' does not establish access to the actual original prompt.

The new candidate at 21:11:32 Beijing time on November 17, 2026 has Chaoji Geti | Shizi (@yaohui12138) introducing @frevana_ai's ReGen Agent App. The poster reports giving it a perfume ad with slow motion, lighting and liquid close-ups, receiving structured editable character, composition, lighting and shot-rhythm parameters within minutes, clicking Regenerate to enter the editor, substituting their own perfume and obtaining a new ad. Retrieval confirmed attribution and the full text, but the demonstration video, input, recipe and output were not reviewed.

The author acknowledges that it is not identical to the original, while personally judging visual tone, shot structure and rhythm close. They promote batch substitution of people, topics, products and languages, and claim image analysis, a Chrome extension and web drag-and-drop access. This adds a concrete usage report, not recovery of the original production process, unique prompt or filming technique, nor guaranteed advertising performance. Minutes, a conventional team's tens of thousands of yuan and dozens of daily outputs were not timed or cost-checked; no definite savings or returns are asserted.
