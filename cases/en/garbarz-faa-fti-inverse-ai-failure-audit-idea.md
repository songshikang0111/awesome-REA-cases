Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · REA use unconfirmed

At 03:01:12 Beijing time on November 15, 2026, a post signed Ariel Garbarz, director of UTN's COMSEC Lab, describes a November 13 US air-traffic disruption. It proposes investigating Chicago and a possible update to FAA Telecommunications Infrastructure (FTI), asking FAA, L3Harris and CISA for update logs, software versions, alarms and failure timelines. The post describes FTI as a communications network connecting air-traffic facilities. The event, contractual attribution and author's position were not independently checked.

The concrete proposal is what he calls Inverse Artificial Intelligence (IAI): reverse automated systems, reconstruct decisions and identify hidden vulnerabilities or failures conventional security may miss. The post applies the idea to a specific communications-failure investigation, but supplies no technical records, inputs, tools, model, implementation, experiment or audit result. It also does not establish that AI performs the analysis. Tool classification remains unknown and status unverified at the idea level; the concept name is not treated as REA or a deployed product.

The author explicitly does not claim an AI attack caused the incident. A Chicago starting point, inadequacy of the router-overload explanation and other common-cause failures remain open questions. Political accusations are not adopted; no attack chain, vulnerability or root cause is invented. Included as a reversing/audit proposal for a specific system, not a completed incident investigation.
