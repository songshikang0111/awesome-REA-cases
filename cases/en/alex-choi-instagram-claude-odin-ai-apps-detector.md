# Instagram's AI-app list: Alex Choi reports a Claude-decompiled odin detector

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-02
Stage: Needs verification

At 17:19:45 Beijing time on November 2, 2026, Alex Choi (@AlexChhk) says a phone was connected to a laptop and Claude Code asked to decompile Instagram. The author reports finding a function named odin_ai_apps_detector with a hardcoded list of 23 apps, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, claiming it checks daily and logs them as 'installed apps.' The research question is whether Instagram identifies installed AI applications.

Original lookup confirms attribution and an image's presence, but that image was not inspected and no APK revision/hash, decompiled code, call sites, permission conditions or logs were obtained. Even an existing function name and static list would not establish daily execution, transmission to Meta or advertising use. Daily checks and logging remain author reports, not confirmed cross-app surveillance. REA is unspecified; Instagram was not run and no private device data was accessed.

Candidate snapshot: 6 likes, 305 views, 0 reposts. The 23-app count and daily frequency are author claims, not independent measurements.
