At 19:54:31 Beijing time on November 3, 2026, Linyi LYi (@linyiLYi) says AI reverse engineering of Street Fighter II led to a browser game called Naiba. Nailong receives three moves named 闪身步, 浪子踢球 and 狗熊哆嗦毛, and a low-gravity moon stage is added. The aim is a creative character/physics remix using an existing fighting game as a reference, not merely displaying decompiled text.

Original-post lookup confirms the author and attached video, but an inspectable project, browser playtest, AI model, tool, reference-game revision and behavioral comparison were not obtained. The author's word 'reverse engineering' does not distinguish ROM/binary reading from gameplay or UI observation. Accurate hit detection, networking, a complete roster and byte-level reconstruction must not be inferred. REA is unspecified.

Retrieval confirms the account and wording, but the linked tutorial text was not obtained. Its full-process title does not establish a ROM revision, tools, hitboxes or frame-by-frame testing.
