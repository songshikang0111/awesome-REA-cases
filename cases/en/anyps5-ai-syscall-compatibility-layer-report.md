Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work

Stage: Needs verification

At 11:02:42 Beijing time on November 1, 2026, a reader compares AnyPS5 with Valve's years on Proton, crediting AI with faster PS5 syscall and compatibility-layer research. No real software emulation is their judgment, not a source or trace result. A November 15 account at 14:58:45 describes native Windows/Linux relinking and graphics conversion to Vulkan, listing syscalls, graphics, encryption and compatibility as obstacles. The 54,000 stars are unchecked; GTA 6 porting remains a prediction.

A November 20 post at 18:21:00 claims git-log inspection found 82 Claude co-author credits in the latest 400 commits, naming Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 over about fifteen hours. A separate video window reportedly shows a PS5-to-Vulkan shader recompiler improving from 52% to 100% over months. These durations do not mean the whole project took fifteen hours; coverage lacks a definition and test set. It also claims Minecraft runs and Wolverine reaches logos, while a clean latest-firmware GTA 6 dump is missing and a pre-launch extraction claim has no files. The GTA VI PC port is already built headline is not acceptance, logos are not playability, and 57,700 stars and Rockstar's supposed 2027 PC plan remain unchecked.
