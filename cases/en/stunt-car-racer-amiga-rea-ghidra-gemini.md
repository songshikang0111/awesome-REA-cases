# Stunt Car Racer: REA hits an m68k limit; the author continues with Ghidra and Gemini

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-10-31
Stage: In progress

At 22:59:29 Beijing time on October 31, 2026, Scientific Brad (@ScientificBrad) reports using REA on favorite 1989 Amiga game Stunt Car Racer. It lacks AmigaDOS and uses an RNC raw-MFM loader. The author mentions XOR-$80 code, checksums and a suspicious 68000 TRACE vector, saying REA hit an m68k support wall. Ghidra reportedly decompiled 480 functions and fixed-point suspension/physics were mapped, with Gemini now working on it. The target is understanding an old racing implementation and attempting reconstruction, not the separate Stunts browser project.
