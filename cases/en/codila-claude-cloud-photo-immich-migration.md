# Cloud photos to Immich: codila describes Claude-assisted reuse of private iCloud and Google Photos requests

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-10-31
Stage: Needs verification

At 05:22:34 Beijing time on October 31, 2026, codila (@0xCodila) describes an integrated Claude Code/Opus 5.5 photo-migration workflow. It checks space, drives and photo locations on a Mac or Windows computer, deploys Docker, open-source photo service Immich and a Tailscale private connection, and calls for an external backup drive. The goal is moving cloud photos to one's own computer while retaining phone backups and remote browsing, reducing reliance on storage subscriptions.

The author calls iCloud original-file downloads and repeating private Google Photos webpage requests the reversing steps. Apple passwords reportedly stay in the local terminal, with Mac Photos or iCloud for Windows offered as alternative download paths; the Google path is said to avoid waiting for Takeout. They also claim N/N checks against cloud listings without deleting cloud originals and phone backups after scanning two QR codes. These are workflow claims, with no actual library size, exported count, metadata/album/Live Photo completeness results or error-handling logs. Equal file counts alone do not establish lossless content, versions or metadata.

A lookup confirmed attribution and text, but the advertised full prompt and repository were not obtained, and webpage scripts, authentication handling and network requests were not audited. Reusing Immich and Tailscale is not Claude reimplementing two cloud-photo products. Private interfaces can change; total privacy, unlimited storage, free operation and readiness to cancel a subscription are unproven. Computer capacity, availability, backups and operating costs still matter. No passwords, account access, script execution or photo migration occurred here. REA is not mentioned.
