# Hellcats Over The Pacific: Matyo rebuilds a 1991 Macintosh flight game with Claude and Godot

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-24
Stage: In progress

At 14:45:35 Beijing time on November 24, 2026, Matyo (@matyo91) introduces a reconstruction of Hellcats Over The Pacific, a 1991 Macintosh air-combat simulator. He says archive research began months earlier, the game now runs in Godot, and a browser demonstration is available. He also worked on historical versions 1.0.3, 1.0.4 and the Leyte Gulf expansion. Retrieval confirms attribution and the complete ending; the demo was not opened.

The author credits ResEdit and Ghidra for resource extraction and Motorola 68000 assembly decompilation, Claude Code for prompted analysis, logic reconstruction and C/GDScript generation, and Godot for the historical environment and modern-platform adaptation. He says he personally wrote no code but understood the architecture, defined the method, decomposed problems, guided the agent and checked results. This is not rewritten as no human work or autonomous recovery of original source.

Three routes are distinguished: Original reconstructs the 68k CPU, memory and Macintosh Toolbox environment; Native progressively migrates to native Godot components; Enhanced adapts to modern devices including Android/iOS and is still being validated. The project explicitly remains under reconstruction. A browser-running report does not establish a finished full game, equivalence across historical versions or mobile playability. No repository, license, asset provenance, differential tests, frame rates or full playthrough records were obtained. No demo, game download or code was executed. REA is unmentioned; in-progress status and author-report attribution remain.
