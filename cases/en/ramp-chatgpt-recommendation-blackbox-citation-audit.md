Stage: Analysis test

At 15:08:25 Beijing time on November 14, 2026, a post offering free audits as lead generation says its team reverse-engineered why Ramp became an AI-recommended spend-management platform. Reversing here means observing ChatGPT answers and cited pages to infer relationships between public content and recommendation visibility, not reading model weights, internal ranking code or Ramp's private systems. It reports Ramp appearing in all 11 tested purchase-intent questions and separately gives Ramp/Brex mention shares of 67%/33%. The denominators are unspecified; 11/11 must not be treated as the basis for 67%.

One concrete probe asks what percentage of businesses use Slack versus Zoom without mentioning Ramp. The author observes ChatGPT building the answer around Ramp's benchmark page and reusing its opening 'As of November 2026.' The post connects this with a reported 317 vendor pages and 38 category pages containing percentages, ranks, update dates and quotable opening sentences. It also reports six pricing-page citations, 14 homepage citations and 18 Gartner Peer Insights citations, plus 13 Brex-related pages, 34 Ramp-comparison pages, 142 tool-comparison pages and 194 card-charge explanation pages. The background of data from 85,000 companies, page counts and citation counts are all author-reported and were not individually checked here.

A counterexample is retained: for accounts-payable automation, ChatGPT reportedly read Ramp's own guide yet recommended Bill, Tipalti and Stampli. The entry therefore records an observed boundary, not a guarantee that certain pages secure first place. A $0 free tier, $15 Plus tier and Slack/Zoom adoption of 56%/71% are page values described in the post, not current pricing or adoption independently verified by this collection.
