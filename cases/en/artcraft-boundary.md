# ArtCraft: not proven Adobe or Office reverse engineering

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-03
Stage: Related project

ArtCraft's website describes creative software written from scratch in Rust, with several products in early testing/development. Circulating posts do not establish complete Adobe reverse engineering. WordCraft, GridCraft and DeckCraft rebuild Word-style word processing, Excel-style spreadsheets and PowerPoint-style slides with corresponding Office formats and MCP; READMEs still label them alpha. October 12 PhotoCraft materials describe PSD support from Adobe's public specification and over 500 commands shared by UI, CLI, JSON and MCP. The reported 307 of 309 psd-tools samples render identically after open/save, not byte-identically in all cases. This is neither a professional everyday Photoshop replacement nor evidence of Adobe decompilation.

A user in the October 13, 04:00–08:00 window criticizes non-floating PhotoCraft panels, opaque color management, missing compression/save options, slow PSD opening, image-focus/mask readings and text controls, finding it unsuitable for professional work. That is one experience, not an official admission or rerun here. An unidentified third party on October 14 claims an REA-like workflow, not author confirmation. October 15 accounts add public format specifications, observed buttons, pixel-render comparisons and independently implemented codecs. At 20:48 a user reports launching all thirteen apps on Ubuntu, relaying a maintainer's HN statement that the team does not decompile, prohibits Ghidra and rebuilds from public information and behavior. Launching is not full functionality. The HN wording, pixel validation and codec scope were not additionally checked and do not guarantee legal compliance or suite-wide compatibility.

A new candidate at 23:44:34 Beijing time on November 3, 2026, says the poster tried Filmcraft and found it identical to Adobe Premiere, while describing all ArtCraft Office/Adobe apps as Claude reverse-engineering results. Only the Filmcraft experience supplements this existing suite record. No version, project, codec, effect or export comparison is provided. 'Identical' is an impression, not feature parity or evidence that every product was decompiled. No independent method, code or new test supports that broader claim. The from-scratch/early-alpha boundary remains; this does not invent a suite-wide reversing success. No software, user project or operating-system coverage was tested.
