# Xiaomi 360 camera: Danilo Dorgam opens the device to obtain firmware for REA research

Category: Hardware and interoperability · REA
Record date: 2026-10-30
Stage: In progress

At 01:23:40 Beijing time on October 30, 2026, Danilo Dorgam (@danilodorgam) posts part two of his Xiaomi 360 camera series. His goal is replacing firmware to stop depending on servers in China and customizing the camera with REA and an LLM. He says firmware is not available for download, and opens the camera to identify its infrared sensor, rotation motor and camera board. Chip markings are meant to help locate firmware, but he reports finding none online.

Direct chip reading with a programmer, firmware extraction and modification are only the next planned steps. No exact camera model, chip identifier, dump, protocol, REA output or successful flashing test is supplied. Disassembly is reported progress; offline operation and customization are not achieved results. Original attribution and text were checked, without frame-by-frame video verification or hardware handling. This collection did not obtain firmware, run code or connect a camera.
