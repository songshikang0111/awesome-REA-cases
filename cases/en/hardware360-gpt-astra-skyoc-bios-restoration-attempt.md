# A SkyOC revival attempt: Hardware 360º reports GPT Astra BIOS comparison and modification, still awaiting tests

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-17
Stage: In progress

At 11:04 Beijing time on November 17, 2026, David Hendrickson (@TeksEdge) describes @360_Hardware using AI to examine ASRock BIOS versions before and after SkyOC removal and attempt non-K overclocking support on his own motherboard. He explicitly lists no confirmed working BIOS, bypass, new clock result or local-AI benchmark. A second candidate at 11:35 says it was already ported to an ASUS Z390, risking confusion of an attempt with success.

Supplementary retrieval of Hardware 360º's Spanish original at 19:05:28 Beijing time on November 16 says GPT Astra decompiled his motherboard BIOS, compared ASRock versions before and after SkyOC removal, found the relevant code and modified his BIOS: 'we will see if it works.' This pre-window original supports progress and model attribution, not a new-window candidate. It names no motherboard model, so the secondary ASUS Z390 claim remains unconfirmed. It explains the old mechanism as shutdown above 103 MHz BCLK and SkyOC falsifying frequency reports; this is the author's historical explanation, not verification here or a rule established for modern CPUs.

The candidate's 2015 i5-6400 increase from 2.7 to 4.32 GHz, about 60%, is historical background, not a result of this AI modification. Firmware revisions, patch diffs, boot records, readback checks, temperatures and stability tests were not obtained; linked KitGuru coverage and images were not read. No firmware was downloaded, flashed or executed. BIOS modification can damage hardware; no procedure is supplied. Status stays in-progress, without claims of restored SkyOC or performance gains. REA is unmentioned.

11:04 sealed candidate: 8 likes, 616 views, 1 repost, 2 replies; 11:35 candidate: 0 likes, 4 views.
The November 16 original had 2,068 likes, 73,337 views, 107 reposts and 72 replies when retrieved November 17. Engagement does not prove booting or successful overclocking.
