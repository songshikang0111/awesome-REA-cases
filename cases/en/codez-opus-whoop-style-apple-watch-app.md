# A WHOOP-style Apple Watch app: Codez reports an Opus rebuild of recovery, strain and sleep features in two hours

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-03
Stage: Needs verification

At 02:53 Beijing time on November 3, 2026, Codez (@0xCodez) reports canceling a $199/year WHOOP subscription and rebuilding the entire app for their Apple Watch with Opus 5.5 in two hours, then open-sourcing it. Listed features include 0–100% recovery from overnight HRV, resting heart rate and sleep; 0–21 strain with recovery-linked daily targets; sleep need and bedtime advice; stress/health monitors, a body-age estimate, habit journaling, on-device AI coaching, and a light/dark Watch app.

The author claims no subscription, account or API key and that data never leaves the iPhone. The text provides a short link and prompt/article pointers; a matching inspectable repository, formulas, device measurements and privacy audit were not obtained. A public repository search found no confirmed match, which does not establish nonexistence. Two hours and $199 are author figures, not full equivalence or total operating cost.

This targets WHOOP-like functions on Apple Watch/iPhone rather than the WHOOP-band protocol, distinct from NOOP/Zhoop and Pranav's ring app. Matching score ranges do not establish official-algorithm recovery; body age and recovery scores are not evidence of medical validity. Original lookup confirms attribution. No app, video or personal health data was accessed for testing.
