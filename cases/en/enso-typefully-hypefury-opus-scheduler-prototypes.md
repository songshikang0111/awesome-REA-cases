# Typefully/Hypefury behavior into four prototypes: Ensō uses Opus for a personal scheduler

Category: Applications and clients · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-19
Stage: In progress

At 22:11:15 Beijing time on November 19, 2026, Ensō (@OxEnsooo) says they pay $99 yearly but use only five features of an X scheduling tool, so they asked Opus 5.5 to study Typefully and Hypefury's external behavior, write a brief/specification and prototype a personal replacement. Both are references for one replacement app, not two independent reconstruction projects. The author says only public pages and reviews were read, not competitor source. Clean room is the author's method label, not this collection's legal or isolation-process certification.

The five features are post previews, thread splitting under X character rules, drafts, a weekly queue and opening-line assistance. The stated rules are 23 characters for links and two for emoji; official rules were not checked. An added checker based on the author's writing rules scores their own sample 37/100 with nine problems. Four designs—Margin, Console, Hook Lab and Week—share the same data for comparison. About 25 minutes and twelve of twelve Headless Chrome checks of typing, Fix, openers and scheduling are reported. A prior run failed because Console's weekly queue was empty and a table broke during parsing. All tests remain author reports; scripts, browser logs and running prototypes were not obtained.

Auto-posting and analytics require paid X API access and are excluded, as are auto-DMs and auto-retweets. Competitor branding and copy are not reused. Selecting a final design, saving drafts persistently on the local machine and releasing it free remain future work, so it is not a complete subscription-service replacement. The full original post was read. Nothing was deployed, no accounts were operated and no target was run here. REA is unmentioned.

November 19, 2026 candidate snapshot: 5 likes, 159 views, 0 reposts, 3 replies. Twenty-five minutes and 12/12 are author reports, not independent measurements.
