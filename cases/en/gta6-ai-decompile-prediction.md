# GTA 6 port ideas: Linux/Vulkan, DVD size and a Qwen/REA PC-version wish

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-07
Stage: Needs verification

At 14:59 Beijing time on October 18, 2026, a poster bets AI agents can reverse about 50% of GTA 6 in three months after an executable dump, speculating the disc requires PS5 firmware that blocks current jailbreaks and delays binary access. No sample or started agent is reported. Fifty percent has no defined function, byte, system or gameplay scope; three months is not measured effort and firmware behavior is speculation, not a leak or PS5 compromise.

At 08:01:38 on October 28, Silver Spoon (@bestfilly) proposes AI decompilation/recompilation and jokingly imagines a GTA VI Linux/Vulkan version on a DVD, with the soundtrack taking much of it. Attribution was confirmed, but code, builds, asset sizes, capacity and compatibility tests are absent. This is neither an executed result nor the earlier prediction author's project. The DVD joke does not establish compressibility of original assets; the older sources do not mention REA.

At 02:52:21 on November 5, a reader replying to @poteto proposes waiting for GTA 6, cancelling Claude and using Qwen with REA to rebuild it for PC. This adds a proposed model/tool route and platform, not an acquired game, started port or collaboration. Revision, sample, repository and playability evidence are absent. Model names do not guarantee capability. Status remains unverified; no game was obtained or run here.

A media retelling at 18:47:57 Beijing time on November 7 attributes to former Rockstar technical director Obbe Vermeij a Rock Paper Shotgun comment about someone potentially using AI to turn the PS5 release into a PC port. He reportedly estimates tens of thousands of dollars in tokens and quality below the official port but sufficient for some players, possibly pressuring an earlier official release. He left the company in 2009; this is not an internal roadmap, implemented project or cost record. The interview was not read. The same post's Q1 2027 PC date lacks an official source and is not treated as a confirmed plan.
