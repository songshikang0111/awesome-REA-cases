# Fallout Van Buren tech-demo reconstruction: Kran gives REA six years of human research and credits earlier decompilation

Category: Games and preservation · REA
Record date: 2026-11-14
Stage: In progress

At 10:32:23 Beijing time on November 14, 2026, Kran (@kran27_) adds context to a Fallout Van Buren reconstruction thread: it builds on @shenmarukai's initial decompilation, which used AI to some degree, though he does not know her exact process. He also supplied his personal IDA database as a reference. Van Buren is an unfinished Fallout project; the target here is its technology demo, not recovery of a complete released game. An IDA database preserves human analysis and program annotations, which must not be counted as discoveries made by an agent from scratch.

In a supplementary 10:31:10 reply, Kran explicitly describes REA plus manual work: he spent six years understanding the tech demo, supplied that research to the agent, and continues guiding progress and tracking legacy dependencies while the agent handles much of the grunt work. Six years refers to accumulated human research, not REA runtime. There is no claim of unattended or one-click recovery.

A supplementary upstream post by Adam Lacko (@lacko_adam) at 04:51:10 precedes this task's window and is background, not a new candidate. Adam credits Kran for workable source, says he ported the graphics code to DirectX 9 and removed Visual C++ 7.0 runtime dependencies, and is currently aiming for a 64-bit build. Source repository, license, function-match rate, build logs and runtime records were not obtained here; completed 64-bit support, behavioral equivalence and complete game content are not established.
