# GTA 6 AI-reversing claims: disputed reconstruction, PC startup and PS5-save compatibility reports

Category: Games and preservation · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-25
Stage: Needs verification

At 05:45:27 Beijing time on November 21, 2026, st1ne (@SolSt1ne) claims GTA 6 material from Cyberleek lacked an executable and contained only data, assets and archives. He says Claude examined existing binaries/libraries, reconstructed entry points and missing parts, and generated a bootable file after twenty hours. He reports reaching the menu and world, occasional freezes and 50–60 FPS on a Ryzen 9 9950X3D with a 5080. He asks whether to upload it and says he has not shared the exe. This is a concrete but disputed claim about rebuilding missing execution logic, not a PC port confirmed by this collection.

Retrieval confirmed attribution and video presence, followed by reading the complete thread returned by the tool. At 06:13:21 on November 21, MechaShithead sarcastically suggests it only plays GTA 6 video with meaningless GUI/telemetry overlays. At 07:11:38, ham likewise questions script windows over video. Others question reconstructing a whole game from assets without an executable; one reports their own Claude refusing. Comments are challenges, not technical disproof. The author's haha reply alone establishes neither an admission of fabrication nor satirical intent.

A separate post at 21:50:20 Beijing time on November 24 cites TweakTown and credits @chasm with extracting prompts from developers' streams. It says the missing Xbox executable surfaced on November 23, with PC settings and startup notices appearing roughly three hours later, and describes clips labeled 2 FPS for a cutscene and 3 FPS for a dark room. It names Claude among AI tools, says an 85%-complete clip lacks a checkable build, and quotes a participant saying nothing is playable and warning against fake downloads. This is a secondary relay of reporting and streams; neither the media original nor footage was examined here. Later community attempts with an available exe do not validate st1ne's earlier claim to reconstruct one from incomplete material, and frame rates from different stages are not a same-condition performance comparison.

Another post at 22:38:33 that day likewise reports only setup/legal screens after roughly three hours, no gameplay, and says port projects including Leonida went offline a day after the exe appeared. It also discusses a 209 GB leaked package, a $15 million meme-coin threshold, Take-Two statements and Anthropic policy. First-party material was not obtained, so file details, legal action and model policy are not confirmed here. The accessibility and stage reports are retained without assigning a shutdown cause or treating all teams as one implementation.

No code, build logs, input inventory, video-provenance comparison or reproducible experiment was obtained. The video was not watched and frame rates were not checked here. Genuine reconstruction, an appearance-only demo, a joke and misleading promotion cannot be distinguished. Earlier comments only contextualize the original claim; new relays are not independent reproduction either. No alleged leaked files, executables or assets were downloaded, and uploading is not encouraged. File authenticity and authorization remain unconfirmed. Figures remain source claims, status is unverified, and this is not recommended as a success case.

At 11:43:21 Beijing time on November 25, 2026, onyx (@onyx_BL) says Claude reverse-engineered a PS5 save and the author saw movement live, explicitly adding that no further information is available. At 11:58:30, Yashank (@yashanktwt), in a GTA 6 PC-progress post, specifically reports Claude-assisted PS5-save research enabling use with an Xbox build, map access on an RTX 3050 and open-world access potentially coming soon. The later post supplies a game context for this kind of save research, but neither identifies the same build, developer or toolchain. Similar accounts cannot merge the projects into an established fact.

These are new secondary claims about save compatibility and startup stages. They do not validate st1ne's reconstruction of missing execution logic without an exe. Map access, movement and a complete open world are distinct. The RTX 3050 report gives no frame rate, resolution or scene and cannot be compared with the older 5080's claimed 50–60 FPS. Retrieval confirms both posters and their wording. Attached screenshots were not inspected, the live stream was not watched, and no save format, patch, original files, commands, build or execution log was obtained. No leaked content was downloaded, save loaded or game run here; status remains unverified.
