# Reconstructing neuronvisuals' stick-figure style: a relayed Claude/Python animation tutorial

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-24
Stage: Needs verification

The candidate at 16:25:46 Beijing time on November 24, 2026 is compiled by S1 Ge and attributed to a November 21 tutorial by Postiz founder Nevo David, with the original approach credited to Mau Baron. It studies Instagram account neuronvisuals' stick-figure format to make new comparison stories, not to decompile its original project.

The tutorial examines ten high-view videos frame by frame, extracting a black background, two rounded white figures, widely spaced labels, an 8–16-second silent story and a winner turning yellow and raising its arms in the final two seconds. Claude writes 30 JSON scenes using WALL, STAIRS and DOORS templates, then Python/PIL animation at 1080×1920, 30 frames per second and 11 seconds per clip, encoded with FFmpeg. A human submits output beside reference frames and corrects one difference at a time, such as limb thickness, label placement or glow. Humans still choose style, review scripts and approve posts.

The relay claims most of an afternoon for initial tuning, 2–3 minutes per clip on a MacBook and about 13 minutes for 30 clips with six concurrent renders. It also relays 228 million views for three reference-account clips in one week and 619,000 views across the tutorial author's 21 clips. Different accounts, periods and workflows are not one experiment; views are neither revenue nor reconstruction accuracy. The complete candidate was read, but the original tutorial, JSON, renderer and account analytics were not obtained. No video was generated or posted. The claimed Fable 5.2 model, costs, rendering durations and business results are unverified. Attribution and unverified status remain; Postiz is only the scheduling stage of this tutorial.
