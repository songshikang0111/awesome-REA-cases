# Nanwan's personal writing-style reconstruction: infer rules from owned text and try an n8n workflow

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work

Stage: In progress

At 18:28:00 Beijing time on November 6, 2026, Nanwan (@snwiki238337) shares a personal writing-style reconstruction approach studied that day: have AI scan a large personal corpus for sentence length, vocabulary patterns, probability distributions and writing choices, then dynamically inject allowed and prohibited rules into generation. Decompilation is a metaphor for recovering style from visible work, not recovering binary code or reading a person's actual mind.

The author reports an n8n workflow experiment, subjectively finding it fairly close to their style but still needing tuning, and insists on human review and revision rather than automatic publication.
