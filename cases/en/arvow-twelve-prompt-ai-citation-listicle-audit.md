# Arvow's twelve buying prompts: tracing AI citations to brand visibility, not rankings or sales

Category: Algorithms and feature recovery · Other AI-assisted work

Stage: Needs verification

At 20:42:59 Beijing time on November 15, 2026, @ArvowCom posts a short-link-only candidate leading to a full article about Vasco studying mentions of an undisclosed client in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok. The target is to recover an actionable visibility path from black-box answers and cited pages, not extract model weights, training rules or prove a search-ranking algorithm.

The article describes twelve non-branded prompts with buying intent, tracking mentions and sentiment. Opening one Perplexity answer reveals four cited sources and listicles. Two interventions follow: publish a similar but better listicle, and, when it is not picked up, pay an already cited site to include the brand. The article suggests Arvow AutoBlog for content writing; this does not establish that all of this client's actual content was AI-generated. Prompt wording and specific sources are blurred in the demonstration, preventing independent rechecking here.

The reported result is about 70% visibility over twelve prompts, with per-model figures from 63 to 78. Monitoring runs every three days after weeks without results, so the overnight or 24-hour framing may mean 72 hours. The article explicitly provides no traffic, leads, sales or ranking evidence. Both interventions ran together, preventing causal separation. This is supplementary reading of the candidate's linked article, not another candidate; no original video was watched and no prompts were rerun. Included as an unverified black-box study of AI-answer behavior, not a general guarantee of manipulating results.
