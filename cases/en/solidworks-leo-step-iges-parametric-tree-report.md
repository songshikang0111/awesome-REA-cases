# SOLIDWORKS LEO: the vendor describes editable parametric feature trees from STEP/IGES

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-09
Stage: Related project

At 22:21:00 Beijing time on November 9, 2026, the official SOLIDWORKS account (@SOLIDWORKS) describes AI Virtual Companion LEO converting imported STEP or IGES files into native, editable SOLIDWORKS feature trees, turning static boundary-representation geometry into parametric models. The intended use is investigating legacy supplier parts without starting from a blank sketch, then adjusting features, dimensions and design intent.

Original-post retrieval confirms the publisher and wording. This is a vendor capability description, not a demonstration performed here or third-party validation. No technical documentation, version/availability scope, supported-feature set, input part, output project, failure record or geometric error was read, and the attachment was not viewed. An editable generated tree does not establish recovery of the original author's actual modeling history or intent, or support for complex assemblies, arbitrary STEP/IGES inputs or manufacturing tolerances. This differs from the SolidCraft alternative-CAD project. REA is unmentioned; neither SOLIDWORKS nor LEO was run here.
