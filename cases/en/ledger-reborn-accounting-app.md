# Ledger Reborn: An Independent Accounting App Informed by QuickBooks Study

Category: Applications and clients · REA
Record date: 2026-11-01
Stage: Related project

A third-party quote post describes Ledger Reborn as an independent bookkeeping app developed after REA/Ghidra research on QuickBooks, covering inventory costs, budgets and reconciliation. It says a maintainer independently wrote the Python/SQLite engine and interface, with Windows and Mac previews generating invoices from saved data, handling partial payments and showing reports. It does not identify the maintainer and is not direct maintainer confirmation.

At 01:30:00 Beijing time on November 1, 2026, a new candidate introducing REA 6.7.0 adds that the creator actually used REA 6.4.0 and Ghidra on QuickBooks Desktop for Mac 2024, investigating inventory calculations, account-type effects on budgets, reconciliation balances and cash-basis reports one behavior at a time. It reports comparing profit and loss, balance sheet and trial balance against Intuit's official sample company, account by account, matching to the cent. The described method asks one behavioral question at a time, constrains specifications with real input/output examples, distinguishes observed/inferred/unknown, and treats even a one-cent reconstruction discrepancy as a wrong explanation.

The new source is still a third-party report without independent audit, inspectable comparison files, an identified maintainer or tests by this collection. It cannot establish all accounting, tax or version compatibility. Version 6.7.0 is the introduction's subject, not the case's reported version. The post warns REA is not a sandbox, runs with local permissions and may send material to model providers; this is an operational caveat, not a safety guarantee checked here. Existing sources and the case ID are retained. Two accounts are not two reproductions; this remains a related project with explicit reported REA use.
