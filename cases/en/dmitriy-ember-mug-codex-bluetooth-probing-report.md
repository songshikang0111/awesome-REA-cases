# Ember mug: Dmitriy reports Codex Bluetooth probing and a control app in about an hour

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work

Stage: Needs verification

At 01:30:57 Beijing time on November 14, 2026, Dmitriy Kovalenko (@0xfflock) reports asking Codex to study the Bluetooth protocol of an Ember temperature-controlled mug and build a control app. He recalls spending four or five days manually researching it six years earlier because he disliked the Android app, achieving command-line temperature control. This time he reports an app in under an hour, also finding light control and a way to display the amount of coffee. The goal is a personal interface to his own mug, not merely redrawing the official app.

He explicitly says he prohibited looking up the existing protocol online and watched Codex probe different Bluetooth characteristics and compare them against a remotely controlled iOS app. This is his description of the method, not an isolation experiment checked here; prior model knowledge cannot be excluded.
