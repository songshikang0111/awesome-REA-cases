# Nikon D2X color firmware: a report of Claude-assisted parameter research and a blue-image test

Category: Hardware and interoperability · Other AI-assisted work
Record date: 2026-11-06
Stage: Needs verification

At 03:19:10 Beijing time on November 6, 2026, Kirill (@kirillk_web3) describes a photographer using Claude to investigate a roughly twenty-year-old Nikon D2X, aiming to bake a Fujifilm-style film look into camera JPEGs. The post says paired RAW/JPEG shots helped identify color parameters: 294 sets of 28 numbers, 8,232 values in total. Claude reportedly built a slider-based color editor with live previews.

The retelling says a new profile was written into v2.0 firmware and its checksum recalculated, after which every photo was bright blue as intended. At most this is a secondary report of an extreme-color parameter test, not accurate Fujifilm simulation, correct operation in every mode or recoverable flashing. Photographer identity, firmware hash, table structure, editor source, flashing logs and RAW/JPEG comparisons were not obtained. '11 minutes' appears as an invitation to watch a video, not eleven minutes of development; the device age is also secondary. Retrieval confirms the promoter and video attachment, not a full frame-by-frame comparison. No firmware was downloaded or flashed here. REA is not mentioned.
